# Design proposals considered — Reggie UI, 2026-09-09

Three independent design agents were each given the audit and a different thesis, then three judge
agents scored them. **All three scored 8/10**, so no single proposal won; the plan is a synthesis.
What follows is kept because the reasoning — especially the cuts and the judges' objections — is
the part that is expensive to reconstruct.

## sharpen  — scored 8/10

**Thesis.** The bet is that the Guidebook idiom — one entity model, one id scheme, a story column of hedged plain English beside a map that never draws more than forty nodes — is already the right shape for all three goals, and that its failures on G2 and G3 are failures of coverage, not of concept. The audit supports that: server-side Core is essentially built and covered by 471 tests, the prose is specific and honest rather than template mush, and nothing in the design is wrong in kind. What is missing is narrow and nameable. The story narrates only committed history (there is no `git status` call anywhere in `src/`, and every derived cache is keyed on HEAD at workspace.ts:270), so the entire window in which an agent is working is invisible. ## change-first  — scored 8/10

**Thesis.** The Guidebook is a static reading of committed history: `RepoCtx.cached` keys graph, facts, history, symbols, services and flows on HEAD sha with no TTL (workspace.ts:270-281; serve.ts:166,170,204,299,322,330), nothing in src/ or ui/ calls `git status`, no route returns a hunk (the only diff calls are `--stat`/`--name-only` at git.ts:175,181 and tasks.ts:417), and the client re-fetches only on `hashchange` (app.js:2296).
