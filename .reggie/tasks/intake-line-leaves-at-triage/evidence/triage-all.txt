# AC16: reggie triage --all in a throwaway repo, run twice.
# Three captured items; the first run scaffolds all three and takes their lines.
# The second run must not print "already exists" for any of them.

$ reggie triage --all      (first run)
Created .reggie/tasks/first-captured-thing-about-the-login-loop/brief.md and took its intake line
Created .reggie/tasks/second-captured-thing-about-caching/brief.md and took its intake line
Created .reggie/tasks/third-captured-thing-about-the-board/brief.md and took its intake line

The brief is the record now: those lines are out of intake, and the task stays ungroomed until the scaffold is filled in.
Fill every section, then: reggie brief lint <slug>
Shape them in a session: reggie launch first-captured-thing-about-the-login-loop second-captured-thing-about-caching third-captured-thing-about-the-board --run
exit=0

$ reggie tasks             (both kinds of ungroomed work counted separately)
  third-captured-thing-about-the-board · probe
    Third captured thing about the board (brief on disk is still triage's scaffold: placeholder text in Why now, Suspected area, Open questions, Not this)

3 ungroomed with a brief nobody has filled in. Shape them in a session: reggie launch first-captured-thing-about-the-login-loop second-captured-thing-about-caching third-captured-thing-about-the-board --run

$ reggie triage --all      (second run)

3 briefs are already scaffolded and still unfilled, so they need a session rather than another template: first-captured-thing-about-the-login-loop second-captured-thing-about-caching third-captured-thing-about-the-board
Shape them in a session: reggie launch first-captured-thing-about-the-login-loop second-captured-thing-about-caching third-captured-thing-about-the-board --run
exit=0

$ reggie capture "A fourth raw one" && reggie tasks
