## Consequence, stated rather than buried:
## The number is defensible as 'specifiers written as a path that resolved to no file'.
## The clause the sentence attaches to it — 'so the map is missing those connections' — is
## an overstatement on THIS repo, because no real import is missing: the map is complete and
## the scanner is reading a test's data as if it were code. The wording is kept, because it
## is right for the general case and the plan chose it knowing where this repo's two came
## from; the scanner defect is captured as its own item.
