# The footer's bail condition, measured rather than argued

The plan bails if "the container footer with the new segment wraps to three or more lines at 1000
pixels wide". Measured in a real browser against the real stylesheets (ui/styles.css + ui/map.css),
rendering `.map-footer` inside a `.map-stage` of the map column's width at each viewport. Height 21px
is one line, 39px two, 56px three. `.map-footer` is `max-width: 60%`, `font-size: var(--fs-meta)`
(12px), and wraps inside the card rather than clipping.

Above 1100px the story pane takes at least 380px and the map column is the rest; at 1100px and below
the panes stack (styles.css:300) and the map column is the whole viewport.

Reading. At 1000px and above, every string the container branch can produce is one line, before and
after. The bail condition is not met and the task does not bail.

The strings above use an unresolved count of 23, which is far above anything measured on a real repo
(this repo publishes 2), so the "after" lines are a worst case rather than a typical one.

At phone widths the segment costs one extra line: two lines at 760px under the tests lens, and at
390px portrait the compound worst case (tests lens plus a two-digit count) reaches three lines of
56px in a card whose bottom-left neighbour is the legend. That is below the width the bail condition
names and it is a crowding question about the floating card rather than about this segment's content,
so it is captured as its own item rather than fixed by redesigning the footer here.
