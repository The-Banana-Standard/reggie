Criterion 16 — the loading skeleton draws the same headings, in the same order, as the payload.

The skeleton comes from sectionHeadingsFor(scope, lens) in packages/reggie/ui/story.js, which
reads the hand-kept HEADINGS table. The payload comes from the story functions in src/story.ts.
If the two disagree, the page visibly reflows by the difference as the story lands.

$ UI_STORY=packages/reggie/ui/story.js PAYLOAD=<story headings> node <scratchpad>/skeleton.mjs
