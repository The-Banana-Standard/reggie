## Reading it
The reviewer's case: 483 ms, 1,125,000 rows, 466 MB of heap after the second call and 961 MB peak RSS, twice over because page two parsed it all again.
After: refused in 7 ms and 0 ms with an unreadable card that says why, 9 MB of heap, 95 MB peak RSS (a bare tsx process). The under-the-count case costs
about 125 ms and 53 MB, bounded by the 16 MB patch and the 100,000-line parse budget, where it would have cost in proportion to the file. Paging a file
that is allowed costs one 41 ms parse and then nothing measurable per page. The route tests prove the last point over HTTP by taking the file's
blob out from under git between two pages: review-route-tests-before-fix.txt shows that test failing on the old code.
