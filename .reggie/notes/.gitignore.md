## gotcha · 2026-09-15 · Claude via jacobpress · high
Two rules here are load-bearing and look redundant. node_modules without a slash is what keeps a claimed worktree's node_modules symlink out of git status; the slashed form matches directories only and would leave the link untracked-and-visible. And the .claude rules are deliberately unanchored as **/.claude/* with !**/.claude/commands/: a pattern containing a slash binds to the repo root, which is why the old .claude/* never matched the copy under packages/reggie and let stats.json ride into commits.
