## how · 2026-09-13 · Claude via jacobpress · medium
Turns any Story into a spoken script: links to labels, lists read as first, next, and last, notes and journal entries say who wrote them, empty sections say why, paths are said as paths. One function for every scope, so anything the page shows can be heard. GET /api/narration serves it; the Listen control and the episode maker both read it.
