## why · 2026-09-15 · Claude via jacobpress · medium
The 'Still to fix' lines on each track are the running record of known defects in the UI, and a task that fixes one is expected to replace its line with what was done and under which slug, rather than deleting it. The record of what was wrong is what stops the same defect being re-planned from scratch.
